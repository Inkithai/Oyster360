# Oyster360 - Demo Script

This guide explains how to showcase Oyster360 to an oyster mushroom farming company.

## Recommended Demo Flow (12-15 minutes)

### 1. Introduction (1 minute)

**Script**:
> "Oyster360 is an AI-powered farm management platform built specifically for commercial oyster mushroom cultivation. Today I'll show you how it helps farms like yours increase yield, reduce contamination, and make better decisions."

---

### 2. Login & Dashboard Overview (2 minutes)

**Steps**:
1. Open http://localhost:3000
2. Login with:
   - Email: `admin@myco.farm`
   - Password: `admin123`

**What to Highlight**:
- Clean, professional interface
- Key metrics at a glance (Active Batches, Expected Harvest, Success Rate)
- Production status visualization
- Environmental trends

**Talking Points**:
> "This is the main dashboard a farm manager would see every day. They can immediately see how many batches are active, what the expected production is, and the overall success rate."

---

### 3. Batches Module (2 minutes)

**Steps**:
1. Go to **Batches**
2. Show the DataTable features (search, sort, pagination)
3. Click on a batch to open detail view

**What to Highlight**:
- Full batch lifecycle tracking
- Timeline visualization
- Growth logs and environmental history
- AI insights section

**Talking Points**:
> "Every batch is tracked from preparation to harvest. Managers can see exactly where each batch is in the cultivation cycle and get AI recommendations specific to that batch."

---

### 4. AI Cultivation Assistant (3 minutes)

**Steps**:
1. Go to **AI Assistant**
2. Ask these questions:

   **Question 1**: "Why is Batch OY-2026-002 growing slowly?"
   
   **Question 2**: "Which recipe has the highest success rate?"
   
   **Question 3**: "What environmental factors affect yield the most?"

**What to Highlight**:
- Natural language interface
- Context-aware responses using real farm data
- Actionable recommendations

**Talking Points**:
> "Instead of guessing, managers can ask questions in plain English and get specific answers based on their actual farm data. This is like having an expert cultivation advisor available 24/7."

---

### 5. Image Analysis (2 minutes)

**Steps**:
1. Go to **Image Analysis**
2. Enter a batch ID
3. Use a sample image URL or describe the feature

**What to Highlight**:
- Health score
- Growth stage detection
- Contamination risk assessment
- Specific recommendations

**Talking Points**:
> "Workers can take photos of their mushrooms and get instant AI analysis. This helps catch problems early before they spread to other batches."

---

### 6. Analytics Dashboard (2 minutes)

**Steps**:
1. Go to **Analytics**
2. Show strain performance comparison
3. Show recipe performance comparison

**What to Highlight**:
- Data-driven recipe selection
- Strain performance tracking
- Environmental correlation

**Talking Points**:
> "Instead of relying on gut feeling, farms can see exactly which recipes and strains perform best under their specific conditions."

---

### 7. Inventory & Operations (1 minute)

**Steps**:
1. Go to **Inventory**
2. Show low stock alerts
3. Go to **Purchases** to show supplier management

**Talking Points**:
> "Beyond cultivation, Oyster360 also helps manage the business side — tracking inventory, suppliers, and purchase orders."

---

### 8. Closing (1 minute)

**Script**:
> "Oyster360 helps farms move from experience-based decisions to data-driven operations. The result is higher yields, lower contamination, and more consistent production. Would you like to see how this would work with your farm's data?"

---

## Common Questions & Answers

**Q: How accurate is the AI?**
> "The AI combines your farm's historical data with general oyster mushroom cultivation knowledge. Accuracy improves over time as more data is collected."

**Q: Do we need to change how we work?**
> "Oyster360 works alongside your current processes. Many farms start by just using the batch tracking and gradually adopt more features."

**Q: Can we use our own recipes and data?**
> "Yes. The system is designed to learn from your specific farm conditions and recipes."

**Q: Is it difficult to train workers?**
> "The interface is designed to be simple. Most workers can start using the basic features within 1-2 days."

---

## Demo Tips

- Speak in terms of **business outcomes** (yield, revenue, efficiency)
- Use the specific demo farm data (Bim Mal Plantation)
- Let the customer ask questions — don't rush through every feature
- Focus on **pain points** they mention during the conversation
