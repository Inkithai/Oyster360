# Oyster360 - Product Overview

## What is Oyster360?

Oyster360 is an **AI-powered farm management platform** specifically designed for commercial oyster mushroom cultivation. It helps farms move from experience-based decisions to data-driven operations.

## Core Problem It Solves

Most oyster mushroom farms currently manage production using:
- Excel sheets
- Paper records
- Manual observations

This leads to:
- Late detection of problems
- Inconsistent yields
- Difficulty scaling operations

## How Oyster360 Helps

### 1. Complete Production Visibility
- Track every batch from preparation to harvest
- Monitor environmental conditions in real-time
- View growth timelines and health scores

### 2. AI-Powered Decision Support
- Ask questions in natural language
- Get recommendations based on your farm data
- Predict yields before harvest

### 3. Quality Control
- Upload images for AI inspection
- Detect contamination early
- Grade harvests consistently

## Key Differentiators

- **Domain-specific**: Built exclusively for oyster mushrooms
- **AI-native**: Not just dashboards — intelligent recommendations
- **Worker-friendly**: Mobile-optimized for farm workers
- **Production-ready**: Enterprise-grade architecture

## Target Customers

- Medium to large oyster mushroom farms (1,000+ bags)
- Farming companies managing multiple sites
- Agricultural consultants and researchers
